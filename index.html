Our Therapeutic Approach

An Integrative & Eclectic Approach

I follow an integrative and eclectic approach to counselling, drawing upon different therapeutic models and psychological frameworks. The approach is tailored to each individual's concerns, needs and therapeutic goals.

Therapeutic Approaches

01
CBT

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Understanding and modifying unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours.

02
REBT

Rational Emotive Behaviour Therapy

Identifying and challenging irrational beliefs that contribute to emotional distress.

03
ACT

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy

Developing psychological flexibility and responding to difficult thoughts and emotions in healthier ways.

04
MI

Motivational Interviewing

Strengthening an individual's motivation and readiness to bring about positive change.

05
TA

Transactional Analysis

Understanding patterns of thinking, feeling and relating through ego states and interpersonal interactions.

06
DBT

Dialectical Behaviour Therapy

Developing skills in emotional regulation, distress tolerance, mindfulness and interpersonal effectiveness.

07

Supportive Counselling

Providing an empathetic, non-judgmental space to explore concerns and develop healthier coping strategies.

Relationship Counselling Frameworks

08

Gottman-Informed Approach

Drawing on relationship research to improve communication, manage conflicts and strengthen emotional connection.

09

Attachment Theory

Exploring how early attachment experiences influence adult relationships, emotional needs, trust and intimacy.

Not sure which approach is right for you?

In your first session, we will understand your concerns together and choose the approach that suits you best.

Why Choose Blossom Counseling

What Makes Us Different

At Blossom Counseling Services, we believe that effective counselling requires more than just listening; it requires understanding, professional knowledge and the right therapeutic guidance.

Science Over Assumptions

Our practice is grounded in scientifically researched and evidence-based psychological approaches. We believe in understanding the root of concerns rather than relying on myths, assumptions or quick fixes.

A Personalised Therapeutic Journey

We follow an integrative and eclectic approach, drawing upon different therapeutic models to suit each individual's unique needs rather than applying the same method to everyone.

Understanding Beyond Symptoms

We look beyond what a person is experiencing on the surface and explore the underlying thoughts, emotions, experiences and relationship patterns contributing to their difficulties.

A Space Without Labels or Judgement

You are not defined by your struggles, diagnosis, background or circumstances. Every individual deserves to be heard and treated with dignity and respect.

Professional Yet Approachable

We combine professional expertise with warmth, empathy and genuine human connection, making counselling a comfortable and meaningful experience.

Mental Health Support for Everyone

We believe counselling should be accessible to ordinary people from all walks of life. Seeking psychological support should never be considered a luxury or a privilege.

At Blossom, our aim is not simply to help you get through a difficult phase, but to help you understand yourself better, develop healthier patterns and build the emotional skills to navigate life with greater confidence.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our counselling services.

What is counselling, and how can it help me?+
Counselling is a professional process that helps individuals understand their thoughts, emotions, behaviours and life experiences. It provides guidance and psychological support to help you manage challenges, develop healthier coping strategies and improve your emotional well-being.
Do I need to have a serious mental health problem to seek counselling?+
Not at all. Counselling is not only for people experiencing severe psychological difficulties. You can seek support for everyday stress, overthinking, relationship concerns, self-doubt, personal growth, life transitions or simply to understand yourself better.
What kind of concerns can I seek help for?+
We offer counselling for concerns related to anxiety, overthinking, depression, emotional distress, self-esteem, childhood experiences, relationships, marriage, parenting, behavioural challenges and other personal or psychological difficulties.
How do I know if I need counselling?+
If emotional difficulties, recurring thoughts, relationship problems or stressful experiences are affecting your daily life, relationships or overall well-being, counselling may help. You do not have to wait until things become overwhelming to seek support.
What happens during the first counselling session?+
The first session generally involves understanding your concerns, personal experiences and expectations from counselling. It is an opportunity for you to share what you are going through, ask questions and collaboratively identify areas you would like to work on.
How long does each counselling session last?+
Each individual counselling session lasts approximately 60 minutes.
How many sessions will I need?+
The number of sessions varies from person to person, depending on the nature of the concerns, individual needs and therapeutic goals. Counselling is a collaborative process, and progress is reviewed along the way.
Are counselling sessions confidential?+
Yes. Confidentiality is an essential part of the counselling process. Your personal information and what you share during sessions are treated with respect and privacy, subject to professional and legal exceptions to confidentiality.
Do you offer online counselling?+
Yes. We offer both online and in-person counselling sessions. Online sessions allow individuals to access professional psychological support from the comfort and privacy of their own space.
What are the counselling fees?+
The fee for an individual 60-minute counselling session is ₹1,500. A discounted five-session package is also available at ₹6,000, offering a 20% discount on the regular fee.
Do you provide counselling for children and adolescents?+
Yes. We offer counselling support for children and adolescents dealing with emotional, behavioural, social, academic and developmental challenges. Parental involvement may be included whenever appropriate and beneficial.
Do you provide relationship and marital counselling?+
Yes. We support individuals and couples in navigating communication difficulties, conflicts, trust issues, emotional disconnection and recurring relationship patterns.
Do you offer career counselling for adults as well as students?+
Yes. Career counselling is available for both students and adults. Through structured assessments of personality, interests, values, skills and abilities, we help individuals explore suitable career options and make informed academic or professional decisions.
Will counselling involve medication?+
Counselling focuses on psychological support and therapeutic interventions. A counselling psychologist does not prescribe medication. If a psychiatric consultation or medication assessment is needed, an appropriate referral can be recommended.
Is everything I share judged or criticised?+
No. Counselling is intended to be a safe and non-judgmental space. You can discuss your thoughts, emotions and experiences openly, knowing that you will be treated with empathy, respect and understanding.
How can I book an appointment?+
You can contact Blossom Counseling Services through WhatsApp or the contact form on our website to enquire about availability and schedule an appointment.
