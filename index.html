Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our counselling services.

What types of counselling services do you offer?+
We offer a wide range of counselling services including Children Counseling, Marriage Counseling, Senior Citizen Counseling, Personal Counseling, Relationship Counseling, Financial Counseling, Stress & Anxiety Management, and Depression Support.
Do you offer home visit counselling sessions?+
Yes! We offer home visit counselling sessions for clients who prefer the comfort of their own home. We also visit schools, colleges, and offices. You can select your preferred appointment type when booking.
How do I book an appointment?+
You can book an appointment directly through our website by filling out the booking form on the Contact page, or by calling us at +91 8779292084 / +91 7744896101. You can also reach us on WhatsApp.
What are your working hours?+
We are open Monday to Saturday from 8:30 AM to 9:30 PM, and on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We try to accommodate flexible timings for working professionals.
Is the counselling session confidential?+
Absolutely. All counselling sessions at Blossom Counseling are completely confidential. We follow strict professional ethics and your personal information is never shared with anyone without your consent.
