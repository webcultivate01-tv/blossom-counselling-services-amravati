Privacy Policy

Terms & Conditions

At Blossom Counseling Services, we respect your privacy and understand the importance of protecting personal information. We are committed to handling the information you share with care, sensitivity and confidentiality.

  1. Information We Collect. We may collect the following information when you contact us or use our services:
    • Name, age and contact details.
    • Information submitted through appointment or enquiry forms.
    • Details voluntarily shared during counselling sessions.
    • Payment and appointment-related information.
    • Any other information necessary for providing the requested services.
  2. How We Use Your Information. Your information may be used to:
    • Respond to enquiries and appointment requests.
    • Schedule and manage counselling sessions.
    • Provide counselling and related services.
    • Maintain professional records.
    • Communicate important appointment-related updates.
    • Meet applicable legal and professional obligations.
  3. Confidentiality of Counselling Information. Information shared during counselling is treated as confidential and is not shared with third parties without your consent, except where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law or necessary to address serious safety concerns.
  4. Sharing of Information. We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary for service delivery, with your consent, or where required or permitted by law.
  5. Data Security and Storage. Reasonable measures are taken to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
  6. Website Cookies and Third-Party Services. This website may use cookies or third-party services such as website analytics, appointment tools or embedded content to improve website functionality. These services may collect certain technical information according to their own privacy policies.
  7. Your Rights. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal, ethical and professional record-retention requirements.
  8. Children's Information. Where counselling services are provided to minors, information and consent are handled in accordance with applicable laws and professional ethical requirements, including appropriate parent or guardian involvement.
  9. Updates to This Policy. This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be published on this website.
  10. Contact Us. If you have questions or concerns about how your personal information is handled, please contact Blossom Counseling Services through the contact details provided on this website: +91 9423425900 or blossomcounselingservice@gmail.com.
