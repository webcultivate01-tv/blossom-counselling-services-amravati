My Mentor

Dr. Chinu Agarwal

Feeling Minds, Agra

Her guidance and encouragement played an important role in shaping my professional journey. Under her mentorship, I undertook specialised training and certified courses in psychological and therapeutic approaches. Her faith in my abilities encouraged me to pursue this path with greater confidence and commitment.

Blossom Counseling Services was born out of a journey that began with a simple yet deeply personal desire to understand people, human behaviour and the emotional world we live in.

My interest in psychology began to grow through my own journey into motherhood. I wanted to understand children beyond their behaviour, explore what shapes their emotional development and learn how parenting experiences influence the people they become. This curiosity led me to explore parenting literature, human behaviour and the deeper psychological aspects of relationships and emotional well-being.

What began as a personal interest soon evolved into a much deeper passion. I realised that understanding human behaviour went far beyond parenting. I became increasingly fascinated by the way our thoughts, emotions, childhood experiences and relationships shape our lives.

Alongside my specialised training, I pursued my Master's degree in Psychology, strengthening my academic foundation and deepening my understanding of the science behind human behaviour and mental health.

With every learning experience, my interest grew stronger and my purpose became clearer. What started as a personal quest for understanding gradually transformed into a professional commitment to helping others navigate their emotional and psychological challenges.

Today, with over 12 years of experience, I feel privileged to support individuals, couples, parents, children and families through different phases of life. My journey has been one of continuous learning, professional development and an enduring belief that everyone deserves to be heard, understood and supported.

Blossom Counseling Services is an extension of that belief.

It was established with the vision of making professional, ethical and scientifically grounded psychological support accessible to everyone. Because emotional well-being should never be a privilege, and no one should feel that they have to navigate life's struggles alone.

And this journey of learning, growing and helping others continues.
