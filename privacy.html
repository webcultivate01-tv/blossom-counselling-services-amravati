Privacy Policy

Terms & Conditions

  1. Introduction. Blossom Counseling respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you visit our website or use our counselling services.
  2. Information We Collect. We collect only the details you choose to share with us, such as your name, phone number, email address, age group and the concern you wish to discuss. This information is collected when you fill in our contact form, call us, message us on WhatsApp or book an appointment.
  3. How We Use Your Information. Your details are used to book and manage your appointments, to respond to your queries and to provide suitable counselling support. We may also contact you to confirm, remind or reschedule a session, and we do not use your information for any other purpose without your consent.
  4. Confidentiality of Sessions. Everything you discuss in a counselling session is kept strictly confidential. Information may be disclosed only when required by law, or when there is a serious risk of harm to you or to someone else, and then only the minimum necessary information is shared.
  5. Sharing of Information. We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or advertising. Your details are shared only with the counsellors involved in your care, or with authorities when we are legally required to do so.
  6. Data Security. We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and to prevent unauthorised access, misuse or loss. Please note that no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we always handle your data with care.
  7. Cookies and Third-Party Services. Our website may use basic cookies and third-party services such as Google Maps, WhatsApp and social media links to improve your experience. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them before using them.
  8. Children's Privacy. Counselling for children below 18 years is provided only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child without the permission of a parent or guardian.
  9. Data Retention. We keep your information only for as long as it is needed to provide our services and to meet our legal or professional obligations. Once it is no longer required, it is deleted or securely disposed of.
  10. Your Rights. You may ask us at any time to access, correct, update or delete the personal information we hold about you. You may also withdraw your consent to being contacted, and we will respond to your request as soon as reasonably possible.
  11. Changes to This Policy. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or in the law. The latest version will always be available on this page, and continuing to use our website means that you accept the updated policy.
  12. Contact Us. If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please call us at +91 9423425900 or write to blossomcounselingservice@gmail.com. We will be happy to help you.
